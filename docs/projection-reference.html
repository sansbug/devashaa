The stack

Everything hangs off one idea: a single date-evaluator that scores each life-area at any moment. The natal chart sets the standing level; the daśā and transits move over it. Read it forward for the near future, backward for the life arc, and perturbed for uncertainty.

the single evaluator

_project_at(jd, ctx) is the heart: given a Julian day and a precomputed chart context, it runs the four-clock ensemble and returns each theme's value. The timeline, backtest, Monte-Carlo and life arc all call it — the same math forward, backward, and perturbed. A parity test pins it to the timeline loop bit-for-bit.

01

Aṣṭakavarga

api/ashtakavarga.py

The classical measure of a transit's potency. Each graha earns bindus (0–8) in each sign; a graha transiting a bindu-rich sign supports, a bindu-poor one afflicts.

The benefic-house tables are fixed Parāśari data, triple-verified (VedAstro, the jyotishganit package, and a 4-agent derivation) and validated bit-for-bit against a golden fixture. Two checksums guard them: per-graha totals 48 · 49 · 39 · 54 · 56 · 52 · 39 and the sarva total 337.

Transit potency śloka

# signed potency ∈ [−1,+1] of a graha transiting a sign
zb = (bav[graha][sign] − 4) / 4 # own bindus, centred on 4
zs = (sav[sign] − 28.08) / 14.04 # sign's sarva, centred on 337/12
potency = clamp(−1, 1, 0.5·zb + 0.5·zs)

Tunable: the 0.5/0.5 split between own-bindu and sarva weight, and the ±1 clamp.

02

Natal matrix

api/matrix.py · build()

The standing verdict — a signed iṣṭa/kaṣṭa balance per bhāva and per life-theme, each a transparent weighted composite of the natal factor web. No new astronomy; it integrates the existing engines.

Bhāva verdict śloka

bhāva_net = .40·lord + .25·occupants + .20·aspects + .15·sthira-kāraka
# each contributor = disposition = polarity(functional) × strength(ṣaḍbala) nudged by dignity
# compose() renormalises over live (non-None) contributors

Theme net blends the theme's bhāvas + sthira/chara kārakas + a signature yoga + the divisional chart (D9/D10/D7…), weights summing to 1.0 per theme (the THEMES catalog). Five bands: thriving ≥ +.40 · supported · mixed · stressed · afflicted ≤ −.40.

known skew

The disposition calc leans on functional-malefic polarity, so some charts sit negative across the board. It's a calibration point, not a bug — the weights and the malefic-polarity magnitude are the first things to tune (see §10, §11).

03

Timeline ensemble + bands

timeline() · _project_at()

The near future is an ensemble of four independent timing clocks, fused into a value + a confidence band whose width is their disagreement.

ClockWeightReadsTier
Viṁśottarī.40running lords that are the theme's significators, ×disposition, ×level (.5/.32/.18)śloka
Gochara (AV).20Jupiter/Saturn over theme houses × aṣṭakavarga potencyśloka
Chara daśā.20Jaimini rāśi clock on a theme house → that bhāva's verdict (mahā .6 / antar .4)jaimini
Double-transit.20Jup and Sat both over the bhāva, its lord and its kāraka — coverage ∈ {0, ⅓, ⅔, 1}, squared so only a near-full transit speakssynth
central = Σ(wᵢ·clockᵢ)/Σwᵢ spread = weighted_std(clockᵢ)
conviction = 1 − spread / 0.5 # agreement → conviction
value = clamp(−1, 1, 0.5·base + 0.5·central)
band = [ value − 0.5·spread , value + 0.5·spread ]

An inactive clock votes 0 (so a lone signal reads as disagreement → low conviction); a clock that can't run (chara with an unknown direction) abstains and is dropped. Chara's sequence direction is set by K.N. Rao's rule — direct iff the 9th sign from lagna is odd-footed (savya), verified against the canonical 12-lagna list.

double-transit — three-target rule & weight tuning · aug 2026

The double-transit clock was upgraded from a binary house-only hit to the strict classical three-target rule: both Jupiter and Saturn must occupy or fully aspect the bhāva, its lord, and its kāraka (a full aspect only — Jup 5/7/9, Sat 3/7/10; the Parāśari ¼/½/¾ partials are excluded, else the yoga would "hit" 7+ signs and persist for years). coverage is the fraction of those three targets doubly-hit.

Its ensemble weight was then tuned by a judge-panel — four independent lenses (doctrinal fidelity · statistical independence · robustness/overfitting · interpretability) scoring four candidate schemes, then an adversary. A 6-chart sweep proved the raw weight is low-leverage (doubling .15→.30 moves the projection only ~1 point on [−1,1]; conviction stays flat), so the real fix was mechanical:

# muted at partial coverage, decisive only near-full
c_trig = bhāva_net · coverage² # ⅓→0.11 · ⅔→0.44 · 1→1.0

Squaring converts the clock's distribution from frequent-and-weak (active ~30% of months at ~0.16) to rare-and-decisive — a bare ⅓ hit is muted to ~0.11, only a near-full triple-target transit speaks loudly. The weight sits at .20 (parity with gochara, rebalanced within the fixed .40 transit budget — daśā stays .60, Viṁśottarī primacy at .40 untouched). The .20 is compensatory, not inflationary: convex muting lowers average firing, so net influence holds near the old .15 while independence improves (squaring lowers trig's correlation with the gochara clock, both of which read the same Jup/Sat pair). The change engine is unaffected — it reads raw coverage (a strict ⅔ rising-edge gate), not the cov² ensemble shaping, so its double-transit event flags don't move.

04

Event-typing

timeline() windows

A window where a theme is timing-driven notable becomes a typed, ranked event.

# a window fires where the timing moved the theme, not just where its base is low
window: |value| ≥ 0.28 AND |value − base| ≥ 0.07
intensity = |peak| × conviction rank by intensity
# cap 2 per theme, 10 total → variety across life-areas

Each event carries its class (theme), polarity, the driving clock, the daśā lords, and the primary house + lord. The frontend renders the localized label + intensity bar + conviction.

05

Monte-Carlo

montecarlo() · /montecarlo

The dominant real-world error is an imprecise birth time. Perturb it and recompute the whole projection to see what survives.

offset ~ Gaussian(0, minutes/2), clamped ±1.8·minutes # default minutes = 4, samples = 160
envelope[t] = p10 / p50 / p90 of overall across samples
event.survival = fraction of samples the event re-fires (same theme · polarity · overlap)
lagnaStability, charaDirStability = fraction keeping the base value
# fixed seed 0x0DE7A5 → a request reproduces

Correctly widens near a lagna cusp (stability drops, envelope doubles) and stays tight for a robust birth. Shown as a second band, separate from the clock-disagreement band.

06

Calibration backtest

backtest() · /backtest

Log real events; score what the engine would have shown at those dates. A personal hit-rate — the honest track record.

for each (theme, month, went-well/badly): v = _project_at(that month)
hit = sign(v) == polarity # the displayed value
hitRate, meanDirectional # on v (base + timing)
timingHitRate, meanTimingDirectional # on central (timing only — base bias removed)

Two scores by design: the displayed value (what you see) and the base-independent timing signal — so a theme's standing bias doesn't mask whether the daśā/transit timing actually moved the right way. This is the surface a future auto-tuner would optimise.

07

Change engine

changes() · CHANGE_SIGS

Transitions, not levels — where a life-area is about to turn, typed across three motives.

DetectorFires when
Daśā junctiona mahā/antar lord that rules/signifies the domain hands over
Transit ingressa slow graha (Jup · Sat · Mars · Rāhu · Ketu) changes sign into a domain house
Sharp swing|Δtheme| ≥ 0.22 over 3 months

18 CHANGE_SIGS (houses + kārakas + trigger planets + mode) group into health · wealth-career · relationships. Direction comes from the driving significator's disposition; conviction = 0.4 + .12·(nTriggers−1) + .22·|disp|, blended with the theme's conviction, deduped within 3 months, capped 2 per signature.

responsibility — by design

The two sensitive readings — a partner's fidelity (rel.trust), loss of a loved one (rel.tender) — are reframed as care-signals ("nurture trust", "cherish and attend"), tagged care:true, opt-in behind a toggle, marked ♥. Never a verdict about another person, never a date or a death forecast.

Turning points are relative — a local peak/trough with prominence ≥ 0.07 over ±3 years (so a life can turn up even below its mean) — plus mahāpuruṣa-yoga activations dated to their planet's mahādaśā. The theme→facet blends are the softest part of the whole engine (see §11).

The gochara layer — Phaladīpikā ch.26, complete api/gochara_rules.py

Every transit line carries the book's own judgment, from the complete 9×12 grid of Phaladīpikā's transit chapter: each graha's favourable houses from the Moon sign (śl.2), each favourable house's vedha (obstruction) house (śl.3-8), and the per-house effect text for all nine grahas in all twelve houses (śl.9-28). The vedha check is live: the engine computes where all nine grahas transit and voids a favourable transit when another planet stands in its vedha house — honouring the text's two father-son exemptions (Sun ↔ Saturn, Moon ↔ Mercury) and counting from the Moon sign (Nārada's method, as the commentary adopts). Rāhu/Ketu carry favourable houses but no vedha pairs — because the text gives none.

# transit_judgment(graha, house_from_moon, occupants_from_moon)
house ∈ favourable[graha] ? → vedha house occupied by a non-exempt planet ?
  yes+clear → favourable yes+occupied → obstructed (blockers named) no → not-favourable
+ phala[graha][house] — the text's effect for that station, quoted in the viewer's language
provenance — the 1946 hindi original

The source is the original Hindi edition — Phaladīpikā (Bhāvārtha Bodhinī), Paṇḍit Gopesh Kumar Ojha's vyākhyā, Motilal Banarsidass, 1st ed. 1946 (a complete library scan; the 2008 English edition on hand is a preview PDF missing ~80% of the chapter — its one surviving Sun page independently corroborates the Hindi table). Extraction ran the same pipeline as the concordance: page-by-page rendering, per-graha extraction from Ojha's Hindi artha (the Sanskrit ślokas are never self-translated), then adversarial verification — 108 of 108 cells confirmed, cited to śloka + printed page. Each cell is bilingual: the Hindi gist condenses Ojha's own wording (native provenance for the Hindi locale); the English gist is a labelled site rendering of the Hindi, §5-adapted. The module is generated programmatically from the verified extraction JSON — no manual transcription sits between the verifier and the shipped table.

Time questions — relative windows & the sensible-age rule _explain_when · _explain_period

Timing windows are relative, never absolute: a window is a stretch where the theme ran clearly above its own average — threshold μ + max(0.07, 0.5σ) over the sensible span — so a strained axis (a children theme standing at −0.10) still shows when it was most activated, and each window reports its delta. Past answers are framed as activation, not a record; future percentages are the clocks' agreement (conviction), never a probability.

Age floors — timing windows below the floor are not shown, and the floor is stated in the answer, never applied silently
children 20marriage 18career 16wealth 16home 16enemies 14foreign 5education 3

The math can show a life-event axis "activated" at age 2 (a daśā favouring the 5th house) — but nobody has children at 2, so the yearly walk starts at the floor and the relative mean is computed over the sensible span only (childhood years cannot bias it). The same floor prunes future windows and change events, so a child's chart asked about marriage gets an honest empty. Both directions of the mortality question — "when will/did … die", about anyone — are answered with the care refusal, checked first in the parser so no other keyword can outrank it.

The chart answers on hover — bhāva cards RasiChart.jsx · SkyWheel.jsx · BhavaHoverCard · useBhavaHover

Hovering any house on the South, North or Sky-wheel chart — on every varga, D1 through D60 — raises a cited card. Touch works too, with real tap semantics: a tap pins the card (with a "tap again — or anywhere outside — to close" hint), the same-house tap toggles it off, and a tap outside the chart dismisses it; on desktop, mouse hover previews transiently and a click pins. The one non-obvious rule: touch pointers are barred from the transient hover path — on touch, pointerenter fires on tap but pointerleave fires the instant the finger lifts, so a hover-only card would flash and vanish on phones. The useBhavaHover hook guards the enter handler to mouse/pen and routes taps through the pin/toggle path with a document-level outside-tap dismiss.

The card itself: Everywhere it shows the bhāva's BPHS ch.11 significations (house-generic, bilingual), its kendra/trikoṇa/duḥsthāna nature, the varga's own read-for line, and the house lord located in the same varga. No backend was touched: the significations, the concordance readings and the server-computed aspects-per-sign were already riding unused in the /api/chart payload.

cite or refuse, everywhere

Each layer keeps its provenance. Concordance readings are cited & dated classical and simply absent when no source covers a cell; yoga effects are cited BPHS ślokas śloka, never paraphrased into a meaning; the bhāva/theme verdict opens its weighted ledger; the life arc stays a broad shape. Time answers refuse what cannot be sensible: pre-birth windows, sub-floor ages, and any dating of a death. The hover cards keep rāśi-chart facts on the rāśi chart and label every varga-frame extension. The layer surfaces what the engines below can source — and refuses the rest.

Limits & how to improve

The honest weak spots, roughly in order of leverage — the best places to put your attention.

1Nothing is calibrated yet. Every weight is a sensible starting value, not a fitted one. The whole engine is uncalibrated until real events tell it what's right. Improve: log your own dated events, run the backtest, and let the hit-rate (esp. the base-independent timing score) drive the weights. An auto-tuner over _CLOCK_W + BHAVA_WEIGHTS is the single highest-value next build.
2The base skews negative on some charts. The disposition = polarity × strength calc lets functional malefics dominate, so a chart can read gloomy across the board. Improve: soften the malefic-polarity magnitude / dignity nudges, or normalise each chart so its themes centre near 0 — the shape matters more than the offset.
3Life-arc facets are fuzzy. The 6 facets are blends of the 12 themes (e.g. "received wealth" = fortune + home + wealth) — interpretable but not rigorous; some houses double up. Improve: compute each facet directly from its own houses + kārakas via the ensemble, rather than blending finished themes.
4Only the change engine uses fast transits. The main timeline still runs on Jupiter & Saturn only; Mars and the nodes are in the change detectors but not the near-future curve. Improve: add Mars/Rāhu/Ketu to the gochara clock, aṣṭakavarga-gated, for finer near-term texture.
5Resolution is coarse. The near future is monthly; the life arc is yearly. Fine events (a specific week) and per-daśā-period life detail are averaged away. Improve: adaptive resolution — daily near-term, per-antar over life — once a curve view can carry it.
6The event vocabulary is tight. ~12 event classes and 18 change-signatures cover the common questions but miss the long tail (litigation, relocation abroad, specific health systems). Improve: expand the catalogs after calibration shows which classes actually land.
7Yoga turning points are partial. Only mahāpuruṣa yogas (which name a planet) get dated to a daśā; raja/dhana/nābhasa yogas colour the whole life but aren't placed in time. Improve: map every detected yoga to its constituent planets' daśās (needs the yoga detector to expose its grahas).
12

Provenance & ethics

Two rules hold the whole thing together.

Cite or refuse. Tiers stay separated — śloka (BPHS), jaimini (Chara daśā, K.N. Rao), synth (the weights) — never blended into scripture. Where a classical rule isn't in the source (the chara sequence direction), it's sourced explicitly, never guessed. Every number opens to a cited, weighted ledger.

Indication, not fate. A window and a direction, never a fated event or a date. The two most sensitive readings are reframed as care and agency by design — offered, opt-in, never asserted.

This is the system as built and live. The design intent behind it lives in the two sign-off specs — the projection engine (🔭) and the change engine (🧭). If you want to improve it, §10 is the dial-set and §11 is where the leverage is; tell me which knob to turn or which limit to close, and I'll build it.
