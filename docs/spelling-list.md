## 7\. Places where the site is not consistent with itself

* Mars is "Mangala" in the default style but "मङ्गल" in Hindi; common Hindi print is "मंगल".
* The Moon is "चन्द्र" in most Hindi text and "चंद्र" in some interface strings.
* "Kendra" is "केंद्र" in the planet topics and "केन्द्र" in the findings.
* "antardaśā" is "अन्तर्दशा" in the daśā reading and "अंतर्दशा" in the walkthrough.
* The five stages are written plainly (Bala, Kumara, Yuva, Vriddha, Mrita) in "Explain my chart",
and with marks (Bālādi avasthā) in the full analysis.
* Yoga names keep the translator's spelling (Maala, Daama, Paasa, Koota, Soola, Koorma, Sasa),
which differs from the style used for planets and signs.
* Star 23 is "Dhanishta" by default; the scholarly form is "Dhaniṣṭhā" (often written Dhanishtha).
